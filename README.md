### 🖥️ Hardware
I design, assemble, and optimize extreme-performance workstations (my own primary rig, *Akhatosh*, being a testament to this methodology). My focus relies on advanced thermal management, BIOS telemetry, and absolute system stability to ensure hardware can endure heavy, 72-hour rendering workloads without thermal throttling.

### 🔍 Digital Forensics & Data Recovery
I specialize in advanced logical and electronic data extraction for **strictly digital storage media** (HDDs, flash drives, etc.). Operating beyond commercial tools, I utilize raw partition mapping, NAND analysis, and hexadecimal reconstruction to rescue data from severe logical and electronic failures.
